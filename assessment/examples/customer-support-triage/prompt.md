# Customer support queue triage

The support lead has received complaints that urgent customer issues are sitting too long in the queue. The team currently works tickets in the order they arrive, but suspects that a small number of tickets should be prioritized differently.

Use the supplied queue export and service context to build a small, practical improvement that helps the support lead decide what to work next.

Your solution should be understandable to a support operator and should preserve the source data. Be ready to explain:

- how you understood and diagnosed the problem;
- the choices and assumptions you made;
- how you verified the result;
- what you would improve with more time.

You may ask the interviewer reasonable clarifying questions before and during the exercise.
